Questions, answered

Can I cancel or change my gift?

Yes — any time, with no notice period. Sign in to your account to change the amount, switch cause, pause, or cancel, or call our team on 877-225-2251 and we’ll do it for you.

When will I be charged?

Monthly gifts are taken on the same date each month, starting the day you sign up. Annual gifts are taken once a year on the anniversary of your first gift. You’ll get an email receipt every time.

Can I pay my Zakat this way?

Yes. Anything given to the Zakat Fund is treated as Zakat and distributed only to those eligible under the eight categories. You can also mark most other causes as Zakat at question three.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Human Appeal Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 87-2410117. Your gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and we send a consolidated summary at the end of each tax year.

What happens if a payment fails?

We’ll email you and retry the payment. Nothing is cancelled straight away — you’ll have the chance to update your card first.

How is this different from The Jummah Club?

The Jummah Club gives every Friday. The Barakah Circle gives every month or once a year. Same causes, different rhythm — and you’re welcome to join both. See The Jummah Club.
